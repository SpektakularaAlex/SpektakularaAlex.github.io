Music

Piano and choir.

Piano

I have played the piano for more than 17 years, including in international piano competitions. I also play lounge piano, and in jazz, funk, pop and rock bands.

I have taught piano for more than five years, including about two and a half years at Svandammens music school in Uppsala.

Choir

I've been fortunate enough to have sung in choirs since the age of five which has shown me the world. I currently sing in Orphei Drängar (OD), in Uppsala.
