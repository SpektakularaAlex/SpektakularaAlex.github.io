§01 About

I’m an Engineering Physics student at Uppsala University, specialising in scientific computing and AI. Interested in applying machine learning and numerical methods to physical systems: accelerators, fusion plasmas and orbital dynamics. Right now I’m working on ML-based autonomous operation of an ion source at Uppsala’s Tandem Laboratory and on an anisotropy project in fusion research at KTH. In spring 2027 I’ll be an exchange student at ETH Zürich. Outside research I teach, represent students in international university networks, compete in hackathons, play piano and sing in the choir Orphei Drängar.
